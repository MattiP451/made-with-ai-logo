# AI-Generated Content Logo

This repository contains an open logo designed for things made with or with the help of AI.

### Design philosophy

The logo features a neutral, retro-inspired robot head reminiscent of the robots imagined in the 1940s and 1950s.

One eye is slightly smaller than the other. This is intentional: a small imperfection that acknowledges that things made with or assisted by AI, much like things made by humans, don't always turn out exactly as intended.

The logo is designed as a neutral, recognizable mark for identifying AI involvement, not as an endorsement or criticism of AI itself.
